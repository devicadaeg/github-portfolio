About Me


Hi, I’m Devi Cadaeg, an SEO content writer and creative learner. My journey started as an SEO assistant, where I learned how search engines work, optimized website content, and published articles on WordPress. Now, I write SEO-optimized blogs and articles, helping businesses and individuals connect with their audience.


With a tech background, I’m comfortable with HTML, CSS, and PHP, which makes website setup and optimization easier. I also incorporate on-page SEO strategies like keyword research, meta descriptions, and image optimization to enhance content visibility.


Beyond writing, I love learning foreign languages, sketching, photography, and painting —anything that allows me to express creativity (though I’m not an expert at them). Recently, I’ve also started exploring video editing, adding another layer to my digital skills.


My curiosity and love for learning drive me to grow and adapt in the digital space. Whether it's writing, SEO, or creative content, I'm always eager to take on new challenges.
